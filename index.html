Codes

Each player's "Copy code" carries their roll, preferences and reroll counts. Paste one or more codes (a party code, or everyone's own codes, one per line) to put them together in one party. The party code also carries the era, the rules and the world seed.

Trade

Two players swap a race, a class (its spec goes with it), a spec (same class only) or a profession (not one the other already has in their other slot).
