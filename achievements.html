Achievements

Every achievement the module can give, with its rewards. They are account-wide, like a roguelite's meta progression: a run is one character's life, lifetime counts every character on the account, and the rewards count on every character of the account. In game: .rach, or the RandomizerAchievements addon (/randach). Pick the content your server runs: some achievements have a version for each expansion (Randomizer.Achievements.Expansion, by default the server's stats era).
